GalleryOS on-device models

GalleryFace.mlmodelc       OpenCV Zoo SFace face recognition (Apache License 2.0)
GalleryCLIPImage.mlmodelc  OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/32 image tower (MIT License)
GalleryCLIPText.mlmodelc   OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/32 text tower (MIT License)
clip-vocab.json, clip-merges.txt  CLIP byte-pair tokenizer (MIT License)

The .mlmodelc folders are generated by scripts/build_intelligence_models.sh and are not kept in git.
Without them the app still works; People and sentence search wait until they are built.
